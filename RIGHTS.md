## Public Assets And Evidence

Public media must have a provenance record that identifies its source type, author or generator, applicable license or permission basis, and redistribution status. A completed record is evidence of the repository's review process; it is not a legal-clearance opinion or a warranty of non-infringement.

Repository-authored showcase renders may be published when their declared inputs are original or separately permitted. Raw third-party screenshots, videos, traces, HAR files, DOM archives, fonts, brand assets, and source bundles remain private unless the relevant rights holder has granted documented publication and redistribution permission.

An operational scan classification such as `public-page-private-research-no-republication` records why a capture was made and how it may be handled. It is not a legal conclusion and does not convert a public URL into reusable public media.

## Benchmark Jury Privacy

Jury records are pseudonymous, not anonymous. A stable juror code can link multiple votes within a run even when the public record omits the juror's name and contact details.

Before recruitment, each benchmark run must publish a retention and redaction schedule. Unless that schedule is stricter:

- contact details and consent records stay access-controlled and separate from ballots;
- public result data uses pseudonymous juror codes and redacts personal, confidential, client, and credential data from free text;
- the contact-to-code mapping and unredacted free text are deleted within 90 days after final result publication or run closure;
- consent records are retained privately for no longer than 12 months after final result publication or run closure;
- frozen numeric votes, redacted rationales, invalidation records, and aggregate results may remain public for reproducibility; and
- an active dispute, takedown review, or preservation duty may pause deletion, with the exception recorded privately.

No run may be described as anonymous unless its protocol and data flow make re-identification by the maintainers infeasible.

## Rights Or Privacy Report

If you own material that appears in this repository, represent its rights holder, or believe public material exposes confidential or personal data, submit a [private GitHub report](https://github.com/ov3rf1w/frontend-design-ultra/security/advisories/new). Include:

- the exact repository path or release asset;
- the source work and a direct source URL when available;
- your relationship to the material;
- the specific correction, attribution, restriction, or removal requested; and
- a private way to clarify the report.

Do not publish identity documents, contracts, credentials, customer data, or confidential evidence in an issue. If private reporting is temporarily unavailable, open a minimal public issue asking the maintainers to enable a private channel, without including the sensitive details.

Maintainers should restrict or remove reasonably disputed public material while reviewing the report, preserve the report privately, document the outcome without exposing confidential data, and update affected releases when practical. A review response is a project-maintenance decision, not a legal adjudication.
