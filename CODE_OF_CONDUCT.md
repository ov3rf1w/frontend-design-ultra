# Code of Conduct

This project expects contributors to be clear, respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the work. Disagreement about design, evidence, or implementation is welcome when it addresses the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, threats, personal attacks, stalking, spam, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or sharing confidential client or security material.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove comments and contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation based on severity and repetition.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/ov3rf1w/frontend-design-ultra/security/advisories/new) and label the report as a conduct concern. Do not publish sensitive evidence in an issue. Maintainers should acknowledge the report, avoid conflicts of interest where possible, protect reporter privacy, and communicate the resolution to affected parties.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, benchmark jury work, and project spaces where someone represents this community.
