# Expected Output: Multi-Industry Domain Distinction

Must include:

- Three distinct page jobs and action paths: confidential matter review for legal, eligibility/comparison/advice for finance, and booking/referral/triage for healthcare.
- Vertical-specific trust evidence: counsel and jurisdiction/process for legal; regulation, fees, methodology, security, risk, and data provenance for finance; clinicians, qualifications, privacy, availability, care pathway, and emergency boundaries for healthcare.
- Different content and interaction risks, including no outcome guarantees, no guaranteed returns or unqualified advice, and no diagnosis promises.
- Typography, data, form, and asset directions derived from each subject world rather than one shared serious-serif template.
- Accessibility and disclosure needs treated as project/jurisdiction inputs, not invented universal compliance claims.
- A shared quality method without collapsing the three industries into the same hero, proof board, CTA, or visual fingerprint.
