# Expected Output: Agency New-Site Directions

Must include:

- A concise Design Read grounded in the hotel's actual materials, rituals, proof, audience, and conversion task.
- Three materially different directions, not three palette variants of one layout.
- An explicit choice of one direction and specific reasons for rejecting the other two.
- A sitewide Brand DNA that can stay coherent while route composition, proof, and rhythm vary.
- A first-viewport contract, mobile recomposition, asset plan, motion limits, content/proof spine, and measurable next action.
- Avoids the supplied prior fingerprint instead of merely swapping its accent color.
- Does not claim cross-project novelty from model memory; it compares only against a supplied history artifact.
- Separates creative ambition from accessibility, performance, responsive, and content-integrity shipping gates.
