# Heart Clinic H-04 — Fixture Facts

PULSE 04 is entirely benchmark-authored. This fixture is not health
information for a real provider and must not be treated as medical advice,
diagnosis, triage, or an appointment service.

The clinic's fictional scope is limited to non-emergency outpatient assessment
and follow-up planning. The request form receives a response within two business
days but does not provide urgent triage. Severe new symptoms belong in the local
emergency pathway. The fixture also supplies practical preparation, step-free
access, a quiet waiting option, and interpretation by advance request.

There are no clinician identities, qualifications, outcomes, reviews, or
affiliations to display. Trust must come from a clear care path and respectful
boundaries.
