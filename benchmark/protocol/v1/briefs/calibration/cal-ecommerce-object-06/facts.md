# Pendant Fixture E-06 — Fixture Facts

OBJECT 06 and its pendant are benchmark-authored fixtures. They are not
real products, prices, legal terms, environmental claims, reviews, awards, or
availability statements.

The pendant has three material choices and three diameter/price choices. It is
made to order in four to six weeks, requires qualified hardwired installation,
and includes a ceiling rose and cable. Configured returns are limited to defect
or damage without reducing statutory rights. A paid fictional repair service
supports replaceable LED modules and drivers for eight years.

The challenge is to make a premium product understandable and configurable with
no product photography. Material, dimensions, price, lead time, installation,
and returns must remain synchronized and accessible.
