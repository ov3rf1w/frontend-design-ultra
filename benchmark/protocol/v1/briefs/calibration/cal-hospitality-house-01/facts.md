# Alpine House H-01 — Fixture Facts

Everything in this fixture is fictional and authored for benchmark calibration.
It is not a real hotel, offer, location, review, certification, or availability
claim.

HOUSE 01 is a quiet twelve-room house fixture in a car-free Austrian mountain village.
It operates in two seasons, includes breakfast and dinner, and asks guests to
stay at least three or four nights depending on season. Arrival happens through
a village shuttle from a fictional rail station. Its wellness offer is limited
to a timber sauna and cold plunge.

The design challenge is deliberate: no photography is supplied. A successful
response must choose an honest visual carrier instead of borrowing atmospheric
hotel imagery or inventing awards and reviews.
