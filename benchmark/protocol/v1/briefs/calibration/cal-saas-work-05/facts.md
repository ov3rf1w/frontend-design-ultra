# Operations Workspace S-05 — Fixture Facts

WORK 05 is a benchmark-authored, non-commercial product fixture. No company, integration,
certification, customer, uptime record, testimonial, or product screenshot is
represented.

The fixture describes a recurring-work system with intake, ownership,
dependencies, approvals, completion, scoped roles, and exportable activity
history. Its sandbox lasts 14 days and contains synthetic data. It is cloud-only
within the benchmark facts.

The candidate must turn these facts into coherent, usable product states rather
than a decorative fake dashboard. All interface data and UI elements produced
for the calibration run remain benchmark-authored fixture material.
