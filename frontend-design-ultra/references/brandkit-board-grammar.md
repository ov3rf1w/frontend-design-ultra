## Symbol Concept Paths

Pick one path before drawing or describing a mark:

- Monogram with meaning: initials are transformed by a real brand idea.
- Product action: the mark encodes what the product does.
- Metaphor fusion: two relevant concepts become one simple symbol.
- Negative space: hidden idea appears through cutout or counterform.
- Construction geometry: grid/proportion system proves craft and repeatability.
- Wordmark-only: chosen when typography is the strongest identity carrier.

Reject marks that are only initials in a circle, random abstract shapes, or generic sparks/arrows without a brand reason.

## Board Panel Roles

A strong board proves a system, not a mood:

- Logo/mark: primary, compact, and monochrome viability.
- Construction: grid, spacing, or proportion proof.
- Color: dominant, surface, text, accent, and states.
- Typography: display, body, numeric/mono, hierarchy sample.
- Component: button, card, nav, form or product surface.
- Image direction: subject, crop, lighting, texture, proof role.
- Application: hero, social/OG, dashboard, packaging, signage, or mobile screen.
- Forbidden defaults: fonts, colors, motifs, layout tropes, proof claims to avoid.

## Brandkit Gates

- Every visual decision must map to positioning or audience intent.
- Board visuals must become tokens or component rules.
- Do not output a moodboard without implementation handoff.
- Do not invent public proof, awards, customers, or metrics.
- If assets are missing, specify the asset plan and use typography/proof systems until real assets exist.
